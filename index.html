What Lumi tracks

Five signals, one calm screen.

Each card glows in its own colour so you can find it without reading. Tap any card in the app to see the last seven days.

Movement

Steps against a daily goal you set, with a nudge when you've been still for an hour.

Default goal 10,000 steps

Sleep

Time asleep and how steady your bedtime is, read from your watch or phone overnight.

Target 7 h 30 m – 8 h

Calories

Active energy burned through the day, separate from what your body uses at rest.

Active goal 500 kcal

Hydration

One tap logs a 250 ml glass. Lumi reminds you if the afternoon goes dry.

Goal 2.5 L a day

The Wellness Score

How 82 is worked out.

Each signal is scored against its goal, capped at 100%, then weighted. The weights below are what the phone above uses.

Movement30%
Sleep30%
Heart rate20%
Hydration10%
Calories10%

  1. Collect

    Lumi reads steps, sleep and heart rate from Apple Health or Health Connect. Water is logged by you.

  2. Compare to goals

    6,320 of 10,000 steps counts as 63%. A resting heart rate in your normal range counts as 100%.

  3. Weigh and round

    The weighted parts add up to the score out of 100, and the trend compares it with the same day last week.
